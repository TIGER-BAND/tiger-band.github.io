TIGER BAND

Korean ballads for a quiet night

Try these two songs on the ride home. In the first, the person being addressed has already left. In the second, they are still close enough to hear every word. In just over six minutes, the same feeling of longing takes two different shapes.

The two-song playlist

Total listening time: 6 min 26 sec

  1. 거짓말이라서, 우린

    2:48 · Released 23 November 2026

    A piano opens this breakup song. Listen for the distance between that restrained beginning and the high notes near the end, when regret finally breaks through. Start here on a rainy evening.

  2. 나만의 그대

    3:38 · Released 23 November 2026

    This love song turns on a request the narrator cannot bring themselves to make. Asking someone to stay might be one more weight for them to carry. Listen with the lyric sheet open and notice that the farewell has yet to happen.

At a café or out for an evening walk

For a quiet café break, start with 「나만의 그대」. For a walk or a late drive, try both tracks in reverse order. The pair then takes you from the fear of losing someone to the moment after they have gone.

Choosing relaxing music for a break

For a short morning commute, the first track fits into two minutes and 48 seconds. Save the longer second track for a walk home when there is time to follow its story. If you usually choose easy listening or soft background music for sleep, sample the ending of the first track before adding it to that playlist. Its vocal builds well beyond the gentle piano opening.
